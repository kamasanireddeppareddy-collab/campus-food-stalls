About

The Campus Food Stall Directory & Review Portal is designed to help students easily discover food stalls available on campus, compare ratings, find useful information and share their experiences through reviews.

🎯 Purpose

One place to see every stall on campus, its timings, prices and what students think of it.

🎓 For Students

Save time, avoid disappointing meals, find budget-friendly food and see which stalls are open right now.

🧑‍🍳 For Vendors

Reach more customers, collect honest feedback and show off popular dishes.

🚀 Future Improvements

Online ordering, photo reviews, real student logins, live queue times and a backend database.
